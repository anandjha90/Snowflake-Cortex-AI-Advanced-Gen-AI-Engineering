# E-Grocery Online Shopping & Customer Service Policies

## 1. Freshness & Quality Guarantee
We guarantee 100% freshness on all perishable products including organic dairy, fresh produce, meat, and baked goods. If any item delivered is expired, bruised, rotten, or spoiled, customers are eligible for an instant store credit or full refund if reported within 48 hours of delivery.

## 2. Order Cancellations & Modifications
- Orders can be modified or canceled free of charge up to 2 hours before the scheduled delivery window.
- Once an order enters the "Out for Delivery" or "Packing" state, cancellations incur a $5.00 restocking fee.

## 3. Delivery & Missing Items
- Delivery drivers will notify customers upon arrival via SMS or app notification.
- Unattended deliveries left at the doorstep are the customer's responsibility after 30 minutes.
- If an item is missing from your delivered order despite being marked as fulfilled on the receipt, report it within 24 hours to receive a direct refund to your original payment method.

## 4. Substitutions Policy
- If an ordered item is out of stock, our team will substitute it with a similar product of equal or higher value at no additional charge, provided the customer opted into "Allow Substitutions" during checkout.
- If the customer declines the substitution upon delivery, a refund will be processed immediately.

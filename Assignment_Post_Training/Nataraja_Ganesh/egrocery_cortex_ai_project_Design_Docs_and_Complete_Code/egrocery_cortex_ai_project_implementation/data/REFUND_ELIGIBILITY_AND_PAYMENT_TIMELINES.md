# Refund Eligibility & Payment Processing Timelines

## 1. Refund Processing Times
- **Store Credit / App Wallet:** Instantaneous (Available within 5 minutes of approval).
- **Credit / Debit Cards:** 3 to 5 business days, depending on your bank institution.
- **UPI / Direct Bank Transfer:** 24 to 48 hours.

## 2. Non-Refundable Items
- Opened non-perishable goods without manufacturing defects.
- Clearance or final-sale promotional bundle items.
- Gift cards and digital vouchers.
